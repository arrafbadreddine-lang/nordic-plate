Authentic Swedish Kladdkaka (Sticky Chocolate Mud Cake)

Klassisk Kladdkaka med Frasig Yta och Kladdig Kärna

Kladdkaka (literally "sticky cake") is the undisputed queen of Swedish Fika. Requiring only 6 ingredients and zero leavening agents, this cake delivers a wafer-thin crackly top and an intensely decadent fudge center.

The 3 Golden Rules of Perfect Kladdkaka

Swedes take their Kladdkaka very seriously. Legend says the recipe was born when a Stockholm home baker forgot baking powder when attempting brownies. The result was pure magic.

⚠️ Common Mistake: Never Use Baking Powder or Over-Whisk

Kladdkaka must NEVER have baking powder or baking soda. Also, stir the batter gently with a wooden spoon or spatula. If you use an electric mixer, you will incorporate air bubbles and end up with a sponge cake instead of a dense, gooey fudge center.

Swedish Kladdkaka Recipe

Adjust servings or switch to imperial units below.

8 servings

Ingredients 🛒

  • 100 g unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 large eggs (room temperature)
  • 2.5 dl granulated sugar (225g)
  • 1.5 dl all-purpose flour (90g)
  • 4 tbsp Dutch-process cocoa powder (35g)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract or vanilla sugar
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Preheat & Prepare Pan

    Preheat your oven to 175°C (350°F / 160°C fan). Grease an 8-inch (20 cm) springform or cake pan with butter, then dust the inside with fine breadcrumbs or cocoa powder.

  2. Melt Butter

    Gently melt 100g butter in a small pan over low heat or in short microwave bursts. Set aside.

  3. Gently Stir Eggs and Sugar

    In a medium mixing bowl, whisk eggs and sugar with a hand whisk for 30 seconds until blended. Do not whip until pale or frothy.

  4. Fold in Cocoa, Flour & Butter

    Sift in cocoa powder, flour, vanilla, and salt. Pour in the melted butter. Fold gently with a rubber spatula until you have a smooth, dark chocolate batter.

  5. Bake for 15 Minutes

    Pour into your prepared tin. Bake in the center rack for 14-16 minutes. The edges will be set and slightly cracked, while the center will still jiggle slightly.

  6. Cool & Serve

    Allow to cool in the pan for 30-40 minutes so the fudgy center sets into sticky perfection. Dust with powdered sugar and serve with whipped cream.

Frequently Asked Questions

When you gently shake the pan, the outer 1-inch ring should look dry and matte, while the center 4-5 inches should still jiggle like jelly. Do NOT insert a toothpick—it will come out wet, which is exactly what we want!
Kladdkaka actually tastes even better the next day! Chilling it overnight transforms the sticky center into a dense, creamy dark chocolate truffle texture.
