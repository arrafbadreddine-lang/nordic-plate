Authentic Swedish Tosca Cake (Toscakaka with Caramelized Almonds)

Klassisk Toscakaka med Knäckig Mandeltopping

Toscakaka is one of Sweden's most iconic coffee-hour cakes. A tender, buttery sponge cake is baked with a rich, bubbling crust of flaked almonds simmered in butter and sugar, creating a crunchy, chewy, caramel lid.

The Secret: The Two-Stage Bake

If you pour the heavy almond caramel over raw batter, it will sink to the bottom. In Swedish baking, you bake the sponge for 20 minutes first until set on top, then spread the warm almond caramel glaze over it and return to the oven for a second bake to caramelize the almonds.

Swedish Toscakaka Recipe

Interactive recipe card with unit converter.

8 servings

Ingredients 🛒

For the Sponge Cake

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1.5 dl sugar (135g)
  • 2 dl all-purpose flour (120g) & 1 tsp baking powder
  • 75 g butter (melted) & 0.5 dl milk

For the Almond Tosca Glaze

  • 100 g flaked sliced almonds
  • 100 g butter & 1 dl sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk/cream & 2 tbsp flour

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Whip Eggs and Sugar

    Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Beat eggs and sugar for 4 minutes until thick and pale.

  2. Fold Sponge Batter

    Fold in flour, baking powder, vanilla, melted butter, and milk. Pour into an 8-inch greased pan.

  3. First Bake (20 Minutes)

    Bake for 20 minutes until the top is set.

  4. Simmer Tosca Glaze

    In a saucepan, heat almonds, butter, sugar, milk, and flour for 2 minutes until glossy and bubbling.

  5. Second Bake

    Spread almond glaze over the cake. Bake for another 12-15 minutes until golden and bubbling.
