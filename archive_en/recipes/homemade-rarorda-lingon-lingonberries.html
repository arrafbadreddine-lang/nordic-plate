Easy Homemade Rårörda Lingon (Stirred Swedish Lingonberries)

Hemgjorda Rårörda Lingon utan Kokning

Rårörda lingon (literally "raw-stirred lingonberries") is the soul of Swedish home cooking. By simply stirring raw lingonberries with sugar without boiling, you preserve the berries' tart snap, vibrant ruby sparkle, and fresh wild forest aroma.

Why You Should Never Boil Lingonberries for Meatballs

Boiling turns lingonberries into a sweet, jammy jelly that loses its brisk acidity. Stirring raw berries with sugar extracts the juice via osmosis while keeping the berries intact. When paired with rich, fatty dishes like meatballs (köttbullar), wallenbergare, or potato pancakes (raggmunk), the acidity instantly cleanses the palate.

💡 Natural Preservative Magic

Lingonberries contain high concentrations of natural benzoic acid. This means you do not need any artificial pectin or preservatives; stored in a clean jar in the fridge, your stirred lingonberries will keep for up to 3 months!

Rårörda Lingon Recipe

Interactive recipe card.

8 servings

Ingredients 🛒

  • 500 g fresh or frozen lingonberries
  • 1.5 dl granulated sugar (135g)

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Combine in a Bowl

    Place berries and sugar into a bowl. If using frozen berries, let them thaw slightly for 5 minutes.

  2. Stir for 5-8 Minutes

    Stir with a wooden spoon until sugar dissolves into a lustrous syrup and coats the berries.

  3. Jar and Serve

    Spoon into glass jars and refrigerate. Serve alongside meatballs, roasted poultry, game meat, or atop morning oatmeal and pancakes.
