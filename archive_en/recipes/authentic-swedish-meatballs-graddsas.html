Authentic Swedish Meatballs with Gräddsås & Lingonberries

Köttbullar med Gräddsås, Pressgurka och Rårörda Lingon

Forget commercial cafeteria versions. Authentic Swedish meatballs are a masterclass in texture: tender, gently spiced with ground allspice, seared in golden butter, and bathed in a silky pan-dripping cream gravy.

The Secret to Fluffy, Tender Swedish Köttbullar

In traditional Swedish home cooking (Husmanskost), the secret to incredible meatballs is twofold: The 50/50 Beef-Pork ratio and the Panade (breadcrumbs soaked in cream until soft). The pork provides juicy fat and moisture, while the beef delivers deep, savory structure.

💡 Chef Astrid's Tip: Don't Skip the Allspice (Kryddpeppar)

The unmistakable Swedish flavor profile comes from freshly ground allspice and white pepper, paired with onion that has been grated rather than chopped. Grated onion melts into the meat, ensuring zero raw crunch.

Ingredient Substitution Guide for International Cooks

Traditional Swedish Ingredient International Best Substitute Notes
Rårörda Lingon (Stirred Lingonberries) Cranberry sauce mixed with a squeeze of lemon juice Lingonberries are tarter and less sweet than cranberries.
Kryddpeppar (Swedish Allspice) Standard Ground Allspice + pinch of nutmeg Use 1/2 tsp for 600g of meat.
Vispgrädde (Swedish 40% cream) Heavy Cream or Double Cream Avoid half-and-half as it may curdle in the pan drippings.

Ingredients 🛒

For the Meatballs

  • 300 g lean ground beef
  • 300 g ground pork
  • 1 dl breadcrumbs (dry fine)
  • 1.2 dl heavy cream or milk
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely grated
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ tsp ground allspice (kryddpeppar)
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt & ¼ tsp white pepper
  • 30 g butter (for frying)

For the Gräddsås (Cream Gravy)

  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3.5 dl rich beef broth
  • 1.2 dl heavy cream
  • 1 tsp soy sauce

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Soak the Breadcrumbs (Panade)

    In a medium bowl, stir together the breadcrumbs and 1.2 dl (1/2 cup) cream. Let sit undisturbed for 10 minutes until all liquid is absorbed into a thick paste.

  2. Mix the Meatball Mixture

    Grate the yellow onion finely. Add beef, pork, soaked breadcrumbs, grated onion, egg, allspice, salt, and white pepper to a large bowl. Using clean hands, gently mix until just combined.

  3. Shape into 24 Meatballs

    Moisten your hands with cold water. Roll mixture into small, smooth 1-inch (2.5 cm) balls. Arrange on a tray or parchment sheet.

  4. Pan-Fry to Deep Golden Brown

    Heat butter in a heavy skillet over medium heat. Fry meatballs in batches for 8-10 minutes, shaking the skillet gently to roll meatballs so they brown evenly on all sides. Transfer cooked meatballs to a plate.

  5. Whisk the Gräddsås (Cream Gravy)

    Leave the flavorful brown drippings in the skillet. Sprinkle in 2 tbsp flour and whisk over medium-low heat for 1 minute. Slowly stream in beef broth while whisking continuously. Simmer 3 minutes until thickened, then whisk in heavy cream and soy sauce.

  6. Combine & Serve Hot

    Return the meatballs to the bubbling sauce for 2 minutes to heat through. Serve immediately with creamy potato puree, pickled cucumbers, and a generous spoonful of stirred lingonberries.
