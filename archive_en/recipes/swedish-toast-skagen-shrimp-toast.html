Authentic Swedish Toast Skagen (Creamy Shrimp Toast)

Klassisk Toast Skagen med Löjrom och Citron

Created in 1958 by Sweden's legendary chef Tore Wretman, Toast Skagen is the premier Swedish starter. Sweet cold-water shrimp are tossed in dill mayonnaise, mounted onto crisp butter-fried toast, and crowned with glowing orange roe.

The Secret: Cold-Water Shrimp & Paper-Towel Drying

Always use wild North Atlantic cold-water shrimp (Pandalus borealis). They are sweeter, firmer, and more flavorful than warm-water prawns. Squeeze and dry them thoroughly with paper towels before mixing to keep the dressing rich and never watery.

Swedish Toast Skagen Recipe

Scale servings or convert units below.

4 servings

Ingredients 🛒

  • 400 g cooked cold-water shrimp (peeled)
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche or sour cream
  • 1 tbsp freshly grated horseradish
  • 1 dl fresh dill, finely chopped
  • 4 slices white bread (brioche or sourdough)
  • 30 g butter for frying bread
  • 4 tbsp bleak roe (löjrom) or caviar

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Dry and Chop Shrimp

    Thoroughly dry the shrimp on kitchen paper. Chop half the shrimp coarsely and leave the rest whole.

  2. Stir Dressing & Fold

    In a bowl, mix mayonnaise, crème fraîche, grated horseradish, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and fresh dill. Fold in shrimp.

  3. Butter-Fry Bread

    Heat butter in a skillet over medium heat. Fry bread slices for 2 minutes each side until golden and crisp.

  4. Plate & Garnish

    Mound shrimp onto the warm toast. Top with a spoonful of bleak roe, dill sprig, and lemon wedge.
