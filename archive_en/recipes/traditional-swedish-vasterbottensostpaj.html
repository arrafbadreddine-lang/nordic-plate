Traditional Swedish Västerbottensostpaj (Aged Cheese Pie)

Klassisk Västerbottensostpaj med Löjrom och Gräddfil

No Swedish Midsummer Eve or August Crayfish Party (Kräftskiva) is complete without Västerbottensostpaj. This savory quiche showcases Sweden’s legendary aged cheese, celebrated for its complex, sharp, nutty, and parmesan-like umami bite.

The Mystique of Västerbotten Cheese

Produced exclusively in the village of Burträsk in northern Sweden since 1872, Västerbottensost is aged for at least 14 months. Legend claims the unique limestone soil and microbial climate make it impossible to replicate anywhere else.

💡 Blind Baking is Essential

Always blind-bake the pastry crust for 10 minutes before adding the cheese and egg custard. This ensures the bottom crust stays delightfully crisp and never soggy.

Ingredient Substitution Guide

Swedish Ingredient International Substitute Notes
Västerbottensost 70% Extra Sharp White Cheddar + 30% Aged Parmigiano-Reggiano or Gruyère Gives the closest match of sharp tang and nutty crystalline umami.
Gräddfil (Swedish Sour Cream) Sour cream mixed with a splash of buttermilk Gräddfil is lighter and slightly more acidic than standard US sour cream.
Kalix Löjrom (Bleak Roe) Trout roe, salmon roe, or lumpfish caviar Adds the classic salty pop to contrast the rich custard.

Västerbottensostpaj Recipe

Scale servings or convert to US customary units below.

6 servings

Ingredients 🛒

For the Butter Crust

  • 150 g all-purpose flour
  • 100 g cold unsalted butter (cubed)
  • 2 tbsp ice cold water
  • ½ tsp fine salt

For the Cheese Custard

  • 300 g grated Västerbottensost cheese
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 dl heavy cream
  • 1 dl whole milk
  • ¼ tsp black pepper & pinch of nutmeg

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Prepare Shortcrust Pastry

    In a food processor or bowl, quickly pulse flour, cold cubed butter, and salt until fine crumbs form. Add ice water and pulse until dough just gathers.

  2. Line Tart Pan & Chill

    Press dough evenly into a 9-inch (24 cm) fluted tart pan with removable bottom. Prick base all over with a fork. Chill in freezer or fridge for 30 minutes.

  3. Blind Bake Crust

    Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Bake the chilled crust for 10 minutes until lightly firm and dry.

  4. Whisk Custard & Fill

    In a bowl, whisk eggs, cream, milk, black pepper, and nutmeg until well incorporated. Spread the 300g grated Västerbottensost cheese into the warm crust, then pour custard evenly over it.

  5. Bake to Golden Perfection

    Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 25-30 minutes until the top is bubbling with golden brown spots and the center is set.

  6. Rest & Garnish

    Allow pie to settle for 20 minutes before cutting. Garnish with a dollop of sour cream (gräddfil), chopped red onion, and fresh dill sprigs.
