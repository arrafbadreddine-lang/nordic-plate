Traditional Jansson's Temptation (Janssons Frestelse)

Klassisk Janssons Frestelse med Äkta Ansjovis och Grädde

Janssons Frestelse is Sweden’s beloved potato gratin. Made with matchstick potatoes, buttery softened onions, and spiced sprats bathed in rich cream, this casserole melts together into sweet, savory, and aromatic perfection.

Important: Swedish Ansjovis vs Mediterranean Anchovies

The most common mistake international cooks make is using Mediterranean salt-cured anchovies. Swedish ansjovis are actually small brisling sprats cured in a sweet brine of cinnamon, allspice, ginger, clove, and sugar. Mediterranean anchovies will make the dish overwhelmingly salty and fishy!

💡 Don't Wash the Cut Potatoes

Do not soak or rinse the potato matchsticks after slicing. The surface starch combines with the cream and spiced sprat brine to create a rich, silky sauce without requiring any flour or thickeners.

Janssons Frestelse Recipe

Interactive scaling and unit conversion.

6 servings

Ingredients 🛒

  • 1000 g starchy potatoes (Yukon Gold)
  • 2 large yellow onions (thinly sliced)
  • 200 g Swedish spiced sprats (Ansjovis)
  • 3.5 dl heavy whipping cream
  • 1.5 dl whole milk
  • 45 g butter (for sautéing and dotting)
  • 2 tbsp dry breadcrumbs

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Sauté Onions

    Sauté sliced onions in 1.5 tbsp butter over medium heat for 10 minutes until translucent and soft.

  2. Cut Matchsticks & Layer Dish

    Peel and slice potatoes into matchsticks. In a buttered baking dish, create three layers of potatoes interspersed with two layers of onions and ansjovis fillets. Drizzle 2 tbsp brine over the top.

  3. Pour Cream & Breadcrumbs

    Mix cream, milk, and white pepper. Pour 2/3 of the mixture into the dish. Dust with breadcrumbs and dot with remaining butter.

  4. Bake in Two Stages

    Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 30 minutes. Pour the rest of the cream over top, and bake for another 20 minutes until tender and bubbling golden.
