Authentic Swedish Semlor (Cardamom Cream Buns with Almond Paste)

Klassiska Semlor med Hemmagjord Mandelmassafyllning och Vispad Grädde

Every winter in Sweden, the arrival of Semlor is celebrated across the nation. Fragrant cardamom yeast buns are hollowed out, packed with rich almond paste, piled high with billowy whipped cream, and dusted in powdered snow.

The Secret: Blending Hollowed Crumbs into Almond Paste

The authentic bakery technique is to blend the scooped-out inner crumbs from the buns directly with grated almond paste (mandelmassa) and warm milk. This creates a luxurious marzipan cream that stays juicy and prevents the bun from becoming dry.

Swedish Semlor Recipe

Interactive scaling and unit conversion.

10 buns

Ingredients 🛒

For the Cardamom Buns

  • 2.5 dl milk (lukewarm) & 25g fresh yeast
  • 450 g flour, 75g sugar, 1 tbsp crushed cardamom
  • 100 g softened butter, 1 egg, ½ tsp salt

For the Almond & Cream Filling

  • 200 g almond paste (mandelmassa)
  • 1 dl warm milk (for blending)
  • 4 dl heavy whipping cream & powdered sugar

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Knead Dough

    Knead milk, yeast, cardamom, sugar, egg, salt, butter, and flour for 8 minutes. Let rise 45 minutes.

  2. Roll 10 Balls & Proof

    Shape into 10 smooth round buns. Let rise 45 minutes on baking sheet.

  3. Bake

    Brush with egg wash and bake at 220°C (425°F) for 8-10 minutes. Cool completely.

  4. Fill & Pipe Cream

    Cut top lid off each bun. Scoop crumbs and blend with almond paste and milk. Fill buns, pipe whipped cream, replace lids, and dust with powdered sugar.
