Crispy Swedish Raggmunk (Potato Pancakes with Salt Pork)

Raggmunk med Stekt Fläsk och Rårörda Lingon

Raggmunk is Sweden's beloved potato pancake: golden and lace-crisp on the edges, tender inside, and served with thick strips of sizzling cured pork and tart lingonberries to balance the rich savory crunch.

The Secret: Grate Directly into the Batter

Never let grated potatoes sit on a cutting board, or they will oxidize, turn grey, and weep liquid. In authentic Swedish kitchens, you prepare the batter first, then grate the potatoes directly into the bowl and stir immediately. The milk and egg prevent oxidation and keep the pancakes golden yellow.

💡 Pro Frying Tip: Thin and Flat

Ladle the batter into the hot foaming butter and immediately use the back of a spoon to spread it thin. Thinner pancakes produce irresistibly crispy edges (raggiga kanter).

Swedish Raggmunk Recipe

Scale servings or convert units below.

4 servings

Ingredients 🛒

  • 800 g starchy potatoes (Russet/King Edward)
  • 1.5 dl all-purpose flour
  • 3 dl whole milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 400 g thick salted pork belly or thick bacon
  • 50 g butter for frying

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Crisp the Pork

    Pan-fry salted pork strips in a large skillet over medium heat for 6-8 minutes until crisp and golden. Transfer pork to a warm plate and keep 1 tablespoon of pork fat in the skillet.

  2. Whisk Batter

    In a large bowl, whisk flour with half the milk until smooth. Whisk in remaining milk, egg, and salt.

  3. Grate Potatoes

    Peel the potatoes and grate them coarsely directly into the bowl of batter. Stir immediately.

  4. Fry Pancakes in Butter

    Add butter to the skillet over medium-high heat. Ladle in about 1/2 cup batter per pancake, spreading it thin. Fry 3-4 minutes per side until deeply golden and crispy on the edges.

  5. Serve Hot

    Serve pancakes immediately with crispy pork strips and a spoonful of stirred lingonberries.
