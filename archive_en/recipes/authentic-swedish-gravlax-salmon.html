Authentic Swedish Gravlax (Dill-Cured Salmon with Hovmästarsås)

Klassisk Gravad Lax med Hovmästarsås

Gravad lax is one of Sweden's greatest gifts to world gastronomy. Silky, delicate salmon is cured for 48 hours in a balanced ratio of salt, sugar, crushed white pepper, and fresh aromatic dill, then sliced translucent thin and accompanied by sweet-tangy mustard sauce.

The 1:1 Salt to Sugar Gravlax Golden Ratio

The classic Swedish proportion is equal weights of coarse salt and granulated sugar. The salt extracts moisture while firming the fish proteins; the sugar balances the salinity and maintains a luscious, silky texture. White pepper adds a gentle warmth that won't overpower the herbal sweetness of the fresh dill.

Swedish Gravlax Recipe

Interactive ingredients scaler and measurement converter.

8 servings

Ingredients 🛒

For the Salmon & Cure

  • 800 g sushi-grade salmon fillet (skin-on)
  • 60 g coarse sea salt
  • 60 g granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp crushed white peppercorns
  • 2 bunches fresh dill (chopped)

For the Hovmästarsås Sauce

  • 2 tbsp Swedish sweet mustard
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar & 1 tbsp sugar
  • 0.5 dl neutral oil & 3 tbsp chopped dill

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Combine the Cure

    Mix salt, sugar, and coarsely crushed white pepper.

  2. Rub & Sandwich with Dill

    Cut salmon into two matching pieces. Rub cure over the flesh, cover with lots of dill, and press the two flesh sides together with skin outwards.

  3. Wrap & Cure for 48 Hours

    Wrap tightly in cling film, place in a dish under a weighted plate, and refrigerate for 48 hours, flipping every 12 hours.

  4. Whisk Hovmästarsås

    Whisk mustard, vinegar, and sugar. Slowly stream in oil while whisking to form a glossy sauce. Stir in fresh dill.

  5. Carve & Serve

    Wipe off curing cure and dill. Slice salmon into paper-thin ribbons at an angle. Serve with mustard sauce, crispbread, and lemon.
