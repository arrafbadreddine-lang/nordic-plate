Authentic Swedish Kanelbullar (Cardamom Cinnamon Buns)

Klassiska Svenska Kanelbullar med Nymalen Kardemumma och Pärlsocker

Unlike heavy American cinnamon rolls topped with cream cheese frosting, authentic Swedish kanelbullar are lightly sweet, infused with aromatic mortar-ground cardamom seeds, twisted into decorative bakery knots, and baked hot and fast.

The Secret: Whole Cardamom Seeds & Soft Butter Kneading

Pre-ground cardamom powder in a jar loses its fragrant citrus-pine oils quickly. In Swedish bakeries, bakers crush whole green cardamom seeds fresh with a pestle. Additionally, soft room-temperature butter is kneaded into the dough at the end rather than melted into milk, creating silky gluten strands that stay soft for days.

💡 Pro Baker Secret: The High Heat Quick Bake

Bake Kanelbullar at 225°C (435°F) for just 8-10 minutes. The intense heat caramelizes the outer crust quickly while trapping steam inside to keep the crumb ultra-fluffy and moist.

Ingredient Substitution Guide

Swedish Ingredient US / International Substitute How to Use
Pärlsocker (Swedish Pearl Sugar) Nib sugar or coarse turbinado sugar Pearl sugar does not melt under high heat, providing crunch.
Färsk Jäst (Fresh Yeast) Active Dry Yeast or Instant Yeast Use 2.5 tsp dry yeast in place of 25g fresh yeast.
Kardemummakärnor (Whole Cardamom Seeds) Green Cardamom Pods (de-shelled) Remove black seeds from 12-15 green pods and crush coarsely.

Ingredients 🛒

For the Cardamom Dough

  • 2.5 dl whole milk (lukewarm)
  • 25 g fresh yeast (or 2.5 tsp dry yeast)
  • 75 g granulated sugar
  • 1 tbsp freshly crushed cardamom seeds
  • 500 g all-purpose wheat flour
  • 100 g butter, softened at room temp
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt

For the Spiced Filling & Topping

  • 100 g unsalted butter (softened)
  • 1 dl dark brown or raw sugar
  • 2 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 4 tbsp Swedish pearl sugar (pärlsocker)
  • 1 large egg for egg wash

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Bloom Yeast & Crush Cardamom

    Warm milk to 37°C (98°F). In a stand mixer bowl, dissolve yeast in warm milk with sugar, salt, and freshly crushed cardamom.

  2. Knead Soft Dough

    Add most of the flour and start kneading with the dough hook. Gradually incorporate softened butter in tablespoons. Knead for 8-10 minutes until elastic and smooth. Cover and rest for 45 minutes.

  3. Roll & Spread Filling

    Beat filling butter, sugar, and cinnamon until spreadable. Roll dough into a 40x50 cm rectangle. Spread cinnamon filling evenly over the surface.

  4. Fold and Slice Strips

    Fold the dough in thirds like a trifold letter. Roll lightly. Slice into 16 strips about 2 cm wide.

  5. Twist into Knots & Second Rise

    Twist each strip, wrap twice around your index and middle fingers, and pull the tail through the center underneath. Place onto parchment-lined baking trays. Proof for 30 minutes.

  6. Egg Wash, Pearl Sugar & Fast Bake

    Preheat oven to 225°C (435°F). Brush with beaten egg wash and sprinkle generously with pearl sugar. Bake for 8-10 minutes until golden brown.
