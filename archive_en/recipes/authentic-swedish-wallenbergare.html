Authentic Swedish Wallenbergare (Veal Patties with Browned Butter)

Klassisk Wallenbergare med Potatispuré, Gröna Ärtor och Brynt Smör

The peak of Swedish culinary elegance. Wallenbergare are cloud-soft patties made exclusively from finely ground veal, cold egg yolks, and heavy cream, enveloped in fresh breadcrumbs and drenched in aromatic browned butter.

The Critical Rule: Keep Everything Ice Cold

Wallenbergare relies on a meat-fat-emulsion similar to a French quenelle. If your cream, eggs, or minced veal are warm, the fat will break and the patties will become greasy and dense. Keep all ingredients in the fridge until the very second they meet the bowl.

💡 Gentle Frying: Don't Burn the Breadcrumbs

Fry over medium-low heat. Because the meat is so tender and delicate, aggressive high heat will burn the fresh breadcrumb coating before the center heats through.

Ingredient Substitution Guide

Traditional Ingredient Substitute Option Culinary Impact
Ground Veal (Kalvfärs) Ground Turkey breast or finely minced skinless chicken breast Maintains the pale color and ultra-soft, delicate protein texture.
Fresh White Breadcrumbs Panko breadcrumbs pulsed in a blender Creates a fine, tender crust that crisps in golden butter.

Swedish Wallenbergare Recipe

Interactive recipe card with unit conversion.

4 servings

Ingredients 🛒

  • 500 g finely ground veal (ice cold)
  • 4 large egg yolks (chilled)
  • 3 dl heavy whipping cream (ice cold)
  • 1.5 tsp fine sea salt & ½ tsp white pepper
  • 1.5 dl fresh white breadcrumbs
  • 100 g butter (for frying and browning)

Instructions 👩‍🍳

  1. Chill Everything

    Ensure meat, cream, yolks, and mixing bowl are chilled thoroughly in the fridge.

  2. Emulsify the Mixture

    In a food processor or bowl, mix ground veal with salt, white pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. Add egg yolks one by one, then slowly pour in heavy cream while running processor on pulse until a pale, mousse-like mixture forms.

  3. Shape & Coat in Breadcrumbs

    Shape into 4 oval patties about 2 cm thick. Gently coat both sides in fresh white breadcrumbs.

  4. Gentle Pan-Fry

    Melt 50g butter in a frying pan over medium-low heat. Cook patties for 4-5 minutes per side until lightly golden brown.

  5. Brown Butter & Plate

    Melt remaining 50g butter in a separate saucepan over medium heat until it foams and turns nutty hazelnut-brown (brynt smör). Pour over patties and serve with mashed potatoes, green peas, and lingonberries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wallenbergare contains only pure veal (no pork or beef), no onions, and uses egg yolks and pure cream whipped into the meat rather than milk-soaked breadcrumbs. They are much larger, delicate, and luxuriously soft.
