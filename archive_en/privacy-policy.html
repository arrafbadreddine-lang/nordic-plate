Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

At Nordic Plate (accessible from nordicplate.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Nordic Plate and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Nordic Plate does not require user registration. We may collect non-personally identifiable log information (such as browser type, time on page, and referring URL) strictly to improve website performance and user experience.

2. Cookies & LocalStorage

Nordic Plate uses browser localStorage exclusively to save your interactive kitchen preferences, such as checked recipe ingredients, completed cooking steps, and metric/imperial unit preferences. This data remains on your local device and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Google AdSense & Analytics Compliance

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you have any questions regarding your data privacy rights, please contact us via our Contact Page.
