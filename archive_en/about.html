👩‍🍳 Meet Head Chef Astrid Lindqvist

Born and raised in Stockholm, Astrid trained in classical Scandinavian gastronomy and worked across prominent Nordic restaurants for over 15 years before establishing Nordic Plate to demystify Swedish cooking for international audiences.

How We Test Every Recipe

  1. Authenticity Verification: Every recipe is built upon historical Swedish methods (e.g. soaking meatball breadcrumbs in heavy cream, crushing whole cardamom seeds in a mortar).
  2. Dual-Unit Precision: Ingredients are measured in both Swedish metric standards (grams, deciliters) and US customary units (cups, ounces, tablespoons) and tested side-by-side to ensure identical results.
  3. Substitution Guarantee: Because authentic Swedish ingredients like Västerbottensost or lingonberries can be scarce outside Scandinavia, every recipe includes field-tested international substitutes.
  4. Google Schema Adherence: Every recipe page embeds 100% compliant Schema.org JSON-LD structured data with accurate nutritional calculations, cook times, and step-by-step instructions.
